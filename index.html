Health Information Checker

Is this health claim reliable?

Every day we see articles, images and videos claiming that a drink cures a disease or a medicine is dangerous. Ask the assistant before you believe or share.

How does it work?

  1. Enter the claim

    Type the text or paste the link.

  2. Review the evidence

    The idea is compared with well-known health sources.

  3. Read a simple explanation

    An easy summary of what is known and what is uncertain.
